Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit the UK is from May to September, when temperatures are mild and daylight hours are long. Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds at top sights.

Travel & Accommodation Options

Option Details Avg. Cost

Useful Travel Tips

  • Currency: The official currency is the British Pound Sterling (£).
  • Weather: Always carry an umbrella or light raincoat, as weather changes quickly!
  • Power Plugs: The UK uses Type G 3-pin power outlets (230V).
